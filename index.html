Questions?

Frequently asked

What's your typical project timeline?

Most projects take 8–14 weeks from kickoff to launch, depending on scope and complexity. We break work into 2-week sprints so you can see progress every two weeks. Custom timelines are available for rush projects or phased launches.

Do you work with existing platforms like Shopify or WooCommerce?

We can, but we typically recommend custom-built solutions for ambitious brands. Custom gives you more flexibility, better performance, and full ownership of your tech. That said, we're pragmatic—if Shopify fits your needs, we'll set it up beautifully.

What happens after launch?

Every engagement includes post-launch support. After that, many clients continue with a monthly support retainer for ongoing feature development, maintenance, and analytics optimization. It's optional — you're never locked in.

How do you approach responsive design?

Mobile-first. Every site we build is designed for phones, tablets, and desktops. We test on real devices, not just browsers. Performance is especially critical on mobile—we obsess over load times and interaction responsiveness.

Can you help with content strategy and SEO?

Yes. We work with copywriters, content strategists, and SEO experts as needed. Most projects include on-page SEO (meta tags, structured data, Core Web Vitals optimization). For full content strategy, we partner with specialists or recommend resources.

What if I need to scale later?

By design, all our projects are built to scale. Database architecture, API design, and infrastructure are all planned for growth. As you hit growth milestones, we can add features, increase capacity, and optimize costs—no major rewrites needed.
